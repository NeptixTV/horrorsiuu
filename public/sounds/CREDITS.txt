Goofy Studio – Pro Sounds: where the samples come from
=======================================================

1) Recorded instruments ("REAL" in the app)
   Upright piano, church organ, glockenspiel, marimba, harp, violin and cello
   sections, flute, trumpet, French horns and the real percussion one-shots
   (congas, claves, cowbell, tambourine, triangle, shaker, woodblock, sleigh
   bells, orchestra cymbal) come from:

     VS Chamber Orchestra: Community Edition (VSCO-2-CE)
     https://github.com/sgossner/VSCO-2-CE  (commit 440300901dfe9275fd84e0b7763af1f8443ae62e)
     License: CC0 1.0 Universal (public domain dedication) – see LICENSE-VSCO-2-CE.txt

     Recorded by Sam Gossner & Simon Dalzell; sample cutting by Elan Hickler /
     Soundemote. Upright Piano and Organ sampled by Simon Dalzell of Ivy Audio
     ("Bearer is granted right to redistribute this sample set by Versilian
     Studios LLC. Credit to both original author and Versilian Studios is
     encouraged."). Thank you!

   Goofy Studio edited the recordings for its sampler: level matching,
   trimmed silence and decays, seamless sustain loops, Opus encoding
   (scripts/sounds/vsco.mjs). The key/velocity mapping follows the library's
   own SFZ files.

2) Synthesized sounds ("SYNTH" in the app)
   All drum kits (kicks, snares, claps, hats, cymbals, 808 toms and cowbells),
   808 and sub basses, the E-piano, music box, supersaw pluck and pad, and the
   FX were generated for Goofy Studio by original code in scripts/sounds/
   (kits.mjs, synth.mjs, dsp.mjs). No third-party samples are used for them.

Everything you make with these sounds is yours – no royalties, no credit
needed in your songs.
